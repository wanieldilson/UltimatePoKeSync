WHY THIS NATURE
Sassy — what you have
+10% SpD
−10% Spe
Treecko's whole point is moving first. Sassy takes 2 Speed off it today and 21 at level 100, and pays for it in a defence a Grass starter should never be relying on.
Modest — what to look for
+10% SpA
−10% Atk
Its Special Attack IV is a perfect 31 and every Grass move it learns is special. Modest doubles down on the one thing luck already gave it; the Attack it gives up is a stat this build never uses.
Nature is fixed at capture. It cannot be trained away — so this is advice for the next starter or the next catch, not a task for this one. Everything else on this page you can still change.
